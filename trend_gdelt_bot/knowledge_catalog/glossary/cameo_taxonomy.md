# Definition

CAMEO provides a hierarchical framework for coding political actions:
- `01–05`: Verbal & Diplomatic Cooperation
- `06–08`: Material Cooperation & Aid
- `09–13`: Demands, Warnings & Verbal Conflict
- `14–20`: Protests, Sanctions, Military Clashes & Physical Conflict
